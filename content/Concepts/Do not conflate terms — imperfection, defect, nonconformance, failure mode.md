# Do not conflate terms — imperfection, defect, nonconformance, failure mode

From [[04 Detailed foundry taxonomy]], section 4.6 (Quality, casting imperfection and causality taxonomy).

> [!important] Do not conflate terms
> A casting imperfection is an observed deviation. A defect is an imperfection judged unacceptable. A nonconformance is the governed failure to satisfy a requirement. A failure mode describes how a process or asset can fail. These must be separate classes.

See also: [[CastingImperfection]], [[CastingDefect]], [[Nonconformance]], [[FailureMode]], [[Non-negotiable distinctions]].
