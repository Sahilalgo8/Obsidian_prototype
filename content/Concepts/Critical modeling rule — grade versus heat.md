# Critical modeling rule — grade versus heat

From [[04 Detailed foundry taxonomy]], section 4.3 (Material and metallurgical taxonomy).

> [!important] Critical modeling rule
> A material grade is an information concept/specification; a heat is a physical traceable quantity. The grade requirement, observed chemistry and actual material state must remain separate so the system can reason about conformance and risk.

See also: [[MaterialGrade]], [[Heat]], [[ChemicalRequirement]], [[ChemicalComposition]].
