# Priority tiers (Axiology)

From [[07 Axiology]], section 7 opening table. These tiers govern how the Enterprise Brain ranks candidate recommendations — higher tiers dominate lower tiers.

| Priority tier | Decision treatment |
|---|---|
| Tier 0 - Hard constraints | Safety interlocks and EHS rules; legal/permit obligations; customer/product specifications; control-system and equipment operating envelopes. A violating option is inadmissible. |
| Tier 1 - Mandatory commitments | Quality release, traceability, customer delivery commitments, approved maintenance and authorization requirements. |
| Tier 2 - Risk minimization | Expected safety, quality, failure, supply, cyber and customer risk under uncertainty. |
| Tier 3 - Economic value | Good tons, contribution per constraint hour, conversion cost, EBITDA/cash, working capital, capital productivity and protected revenue. |
| Tier 4 - Sustainability and workforce | Energy/carbon/water/waste, exposure, ergonomics, workload, learning and resilience. |
| Tier 5 - Learning value | Information gained, repeatability, cross-site reuse and reduction of future decision time. |
